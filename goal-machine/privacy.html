🔐 Privacy policy

Goal Machine, by Opportunistic Games. Last updated 26 September 2026.

The short version

Goal Machine doesn't track you, show ads or use analytics. It keeps your games on your own device. The only things that leave it are the name, scores and optional profile picture you choose to share, backups you make, and the moves in online games.

What stays on your device

Your scores, album, streaks, daily results, settings, favourite club and any game in progress are saved in your browser or app storage on your phone. They never leave it unless you use a feature below. Clearing the app's data or your browser's site data deletes them.

What we store on our server

  • Leaderboard name and scores. When a game finishes, the score, game mode and date are posted under your leaderboard name, and anyone can see them on the leaderboards. Names can't contain offensive words, and anyone with a leaderboard name can report one. A name reported by several players is hidden from the leaderboards until its owner changes it in Settings (your scores, friends and backup move with you).
  • Your account key. When you claim a name, your phone makes a random secret key. We store only a scrambled version of it (a hash), which proves scores come from you. We don't ask for your email, phone number or password.
  • Profile picture (optional). If you add one, a small copy (160×160) is stored with your name. Your friends and the people you play online see it; it isn't shown on the public leaderboards. Anyone can report a picture as offensive, and pictures reported by several players are removed. Remove yours any time in Settings.
  • Backups. If you have an account, your album, stats, streaks and scores from this device are backed up under your name so you can restore them on another phone. Only you (with your account key) can read them.
  • Online games. A game room holds the two players' names and their picks or running totals. Rooms are deleted two days after they were last used.

This data is held by our database provider, Supabase, in the UK (London). We don't sell or share it, and we don't use it for advertising.

Other services the game uses

Player photos are loaded from the Premier League, Transfermarkt or Wikimedia Commons, and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those servers see the usual technical details of a request (such as your IP address). We send them nothing about you.

Children

Goal Machine is a general-audience football quiz and isn't directed at children under 13. The only personal information it can hold is the leaderboard name you choose, so please don't use your full real name.

Your choices

  • You can play without a leaderboard name. Just tap “Not now” when asked, and nothing is posted.
  • Delete your account yourself in ⚙️ Settings → Account → Delete my account: your name, scores, friends, online games and backup are removed from the server straight away.
  • Or, to have your leaderboard name and scores removed for you, open an issue at github.com/OpportunisticGames, or email the address on the Google Play listing, with your leaderboard name. We'll delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Goal Machine is a fan-made game and isn't affiliated with the Premier League, its clubs or FourFourTwo.
